Dear Bureau of Meteorology,

I am a Master of Information Technology student at Charles Darwin University in
Darwin. I am building a free, non-commercial web app called CommunityConnectNT
that shows Northern Territory warnings alongside shelter locations and emergency
contacts, translated into the languages commonly spoken in the Top End.

At the moment I read NT warning products from your anonymous FTP service
(ftp.bom.gov.au, /anon/gen/fwo, the IDD products) using a scheduled script that
runs every ten minutes. I understand from the anonymous FTP user guide that this
service is provided for personal and internal use, and that anyone intending to
publish Bureau data should do so as a Registered User.

The app is now published at:
https://subediparku-eng.github.io/communityconnectnt/

I would like to make sure I am using your data correctly. Could you please tell
me:

1. Whether Registered User status is the right path for a project like this,
   and how I apply.
2. Whether there is any cost for non-commercial or student use.
3. What attribution you would like displayed alongside the warnings. At present
   each warning is labelled "Bureau of Meteorology" and links back to
   bom.gov.au, and the app states clearly that it is a prototype and not an
   official emergency service.
4. Whether a ten-minute polling interval on the FTP service is acceptable, or
   whether you would prefer a different interval or a different feed.

I would rather fix anything that is not right before more people use it. I am
happy to change the polling frequency, the attribution, or the data source if
that helps.
